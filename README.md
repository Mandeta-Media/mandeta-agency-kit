## How the Partnership Works

Choose the model that fits your agency:

### Self-Service
Best for agencies with internal media teams.  
You receive full DSP access and manage campaigns yourself — 
full control, maximum margin, no management overhead.

### Managed Service
Best for agencies without a dedicated programmatic trader.  
Send us the brief and creatives. Mandeta executes, optimizes, 
and reports the full campaign end to end. You stay in control 
of the client relationship.

Both models give you access to the same premium inventory, 
technology, and reporting dashboard.
